Find the safe, accessible path around campus.

Accessible Campus Navigator is a mobile application that helps wheelchair users and visually impaired students find classrooms, libraries and other buildings using accessible routes.

Our aim: make travelling around campus easy and safe for everyone.

Try the route demo

What is the problem?

Students don't know which path is safe and accessible for them. These are the barriers they face on campus:

🪜

Stairs

Wheelchair users cannot easily use stairs.

🛗

Unknown ramps & lifts

Students don't know where ramps and lifts are available.

🔎

Finding classrooms

Visually impaired students may find it difficult to find classrooms.

⚠️

Broken lifts

Sometimes lifts stop working, and students don't know about it.

What is our solution?

We are developing a mobile application that shows students the correct path to reach their destination. The app provides:

  • Fastest route – the route that takes less time.
  • Step-free route – a route without stairs.
  • Voice directions – the app tells students where to go.
  • Landmark directions – instructions using places like reception, lifts and entrances.
  • Report broken lift – students can report if a lift is not working.
  • Route updates – the app suggests another route if a lift is broken.

Main features

1

Two route options

Choose the fastest route or a step-free route.

2

Voice navigation

The app speaks directions so visually impaired students can follow them.

3

Landmark directions

Directions use familiar places like the main gate, reception and lift.

4

Report broken lift

Students can inform others when a lift is not working.

5

Route update

If a lift is broken, the app finds another accessible route after the issue is verified.

Lift status – Block A Lift

Everyone should be able to move around campus with confidence. Accessible Campus Navigator gives every student a route they can trust.
